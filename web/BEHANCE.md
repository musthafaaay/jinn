# Behance project draft

## Project title

**Motion / Film — Selected Editing & Motion Work**

## Short description

Selected video editing, motion design, compositing, and colour work. Built around pace, detail, and a clear point of view.

## Project story

This portfolio brings together nine short pieces across After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, and Premiere Pro. They explore post-production through 3D motion, animated ads, speed and rhythm, shot matching, colour, product editing, green-screen compositing, and content editing.

### Suggested upload order

1. **Cover:** a clean still from the strongest finished clip, with “Motion / Film — Selected Work” set in large type.
2. **Opening:** one short showreel that introduces the overall pace and visual tone.
3. **Spatial Forms:** After Effects 3D study.
4. **Colour in Layers:** DaVinci Resolve grade and masking.
5. **Soft Focus:** animated ad from After Effects.
6. **Speed / Shift:** speed-ramp study.
7. **One Scene, Two Looks:** DaVinci Resolve shot match.
8. **Product in Motion:** Premiere Pro advertisement.
9. **Cut to the Beat:** DaVinci Resolve rhythm edit.
10. **Everyday Stories:** Premiere Pro content edit.
11. **Shape the Scene:** Premiere Pro green-screen keying and compositing.
12. **End card:** “Video editing · Motion design · Colour” and your contact address.
